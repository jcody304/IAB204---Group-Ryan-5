Acknowledgement of Country

Learn how to create a respectful and meaningful Acknowledgement of Country for your event.

What is an Acknowledgement of Country?

An Acknowledgement of Country recognises the Traditional Custodians of the land where an event takes place and acknowledges their ongoing connection to Country, culture, community, and traditions.

Acknowledgements of Country are an important part of reconciliation in Australia and show respect to Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples.

Difference Between a Welcome and an Acknowledgement

A Welcome to Country is performed by a Traditional Owner or Custodian of the specific Country where the event takes place.

An Acknowledgement of Country can be given by any person respectfully and does not require them to be Indigenous.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Avoid guessing Traditional Custodians without reliable research.
  • Avoid confusing a Welcome to Country with an Acknowledgement of Country.
  • Avoid tokenistic or generic statements where a more meaningful statement is appropriate.
  • Be aware that some Country boundaries or Traditional Owner groups may be contested.
  • Ensure respectful and culturally appropriate language is used.
