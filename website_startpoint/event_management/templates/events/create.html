Acknowledgement of Country Guidance

An Acknowledgement of Country is a respectful statement that recognises the Traditional Custodians or Traditional Owners of the land where an event takes place.

A Welcome to Country is different. A Welcome to Country is delivered by a Traditional Owner or Custodian of that specific Country. An Acknowledgement of Country can be given by any person when done respectfully.

Helpful research links

  • AIATSIS Map of Indigenous Australia
  • Reconciliation Australia guidance
  • Local Aboriginal Land Councils
  • State or Territory Aboriginal affairs website
  • Local council Acknowledgement guidance

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not guess the Traditional Custodians without checking reliable sources.
  • Do not call it a Welcome to Country unless it is delivered by an appropriate Traditional Owner.
  • Do not use a generic statement if your event needs a more place-specific acknowledgement.
  • Do not make the statement tokenistic. It should show genuine respect and awareness.
  • Be careful where Country, language groups, boundaries, or custodianship may be contested.
